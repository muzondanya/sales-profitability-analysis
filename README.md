# sales-profitability-analysis

Cleaned and merged three datasets (1,328 orders, 45 countries, 12 product categories) in Python/Pandas, resolving 6.17% missing data and validating for date and code anomalies. 
Analyzed $501M in total profit across categories, regions, and sales channels, finding Europe generated 95% of profit ($476M) versus Asia's $26M, and that offline and online channels contributed an almost even split (51% vs 49%) of revenue.
